Your data

Koi has no telemetry, no crash reporting and no studies. That code is not switched off in a setting — it is left out of the build, so there is nothing to turn back on. Features Koi inherits from Firefox, such as search suggestions and safe-browsing protection, still reach their own providers; both can be turned off in Settings.
